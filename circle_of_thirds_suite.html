What is the Circle of Thirds?

While the traditional Circle of Fifths organizes the 12 chromatic notes by steps of 7 semitones (Perfect 5ths) to establish diatonic key signatures, the Circle of Thirds organizes musical space by interval steps of 3 semitones (Minor 3rds) or 4 semitones (Major 3rds).

By moving in thirds rather than fifths, harmony breaks free from traditional root movement (like V - I cadences) and enters symmetrical pitch space. This forms the foundation for modern jazz modulation, romantic era classical composition, and cinematic film scoring.

2

The Symmetrical Geometry of Pitch Space

The 12-tone equal temperament system can be evenly divided into geometric polygons based on thirds:

Major 3rd Cycles (Equilateral Triangles)

4 semitones × 3 steps = 12 semitones (1 Octave). Forms 4 distinct augmented triad families (e.g. C - E - G#).

Minor 3rd Cycles (Squares)

3 semitones × 4 steps = 12 semitones (1 Octave). Forms 3 distinct diminished 7th families (e.g. C - Eb - Gb - A).

3

Coltrane Changes & Harmonic Substitution

In 1959, saxophonist John Coltrane revolutionized jazz harmony with his composition "Giant Steps". Coltrane substituted standard ii - V - I progressions with key centers moving rapidly in major thirds.

The Giant Steps Matrix (3 Tonal Centers: B, G, Eb)
| Bmaj7 D7 | Gmaj7 Bb7 | Ebmaj7 | A-7 D7 | | Gmaj7 Bb7 | Ebmaj7 F#7 | Bmaj7 | F#-7 B7 |

Notice how the major chords (Bmaj7 → Gmaj7 → Ebmaj7) outline a major 3rd cycle, dividing the octave into three equal points.

4

Chromatic Mediants in Film Scoring

A chromatic mediant relationship occurs between two chords whose roots are a major or minor 3rd apart and share the same chord quality (e.g., C Major to E Major, or C Major to Ab Major).

Because these chords share exactly one voice-led note (the common tone) while changing key centers drastically, they produce a sense of wonder, heroic triumph, or mysterious depth. This is heavily utilized in film scores like Star Wars, Inception, and Lord of the Rings.

5

Guitar Fretboard Implementation

On guitar, major 3rds move 4 frets along a single string (or diagonal shapes across adjacent strings). Because the B string is tuned a major 3rd above G, major 3rd cycles create clean vertical stack patterns across strings 3 and 2!

Practice Routines & Exercises

Drill 1: Augmented Triad String Sweeps

Play C - E - G# arpeggios up the neck, shifting up 4 frets for each inversion on the top 3 strings.

Drill 2: Diminished 7th Slide (Minor 3rds)

Take a standard Diminished 7th shape and shift it 3 frets up continuously. Notice how the notes remain identical!
